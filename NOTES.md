# NOTES — news research, Wednesday 18 November 2026

Researched ~01:15–01:30 UTC on 18 Nov 2026 (08:15–08:30 WIB). Only stories
already reported by then are used; nothing later in the day is invented.
Each story is verified against at least two independent sources. All six
were the lead headlines of the 18 November morning editions worldwide
(reported overnight 17→18 Nov UTC, i.e. on 18 Nov in Asia-Pacific time
zones, and carried in 18-Nov news roundups).

## 1. Trump in Manila for Quad summit
- US President Donald Trump arrived in Manila on Tuesday 17 Nov; first
  Quad leaders' meeting (US, Japan, India, Australia) in two years.
  Bilateral talks with President Ferdinand Marcos Jr. scheduled for
  Wednesday 18 Nov, then on to Shenzhen for APEC and a historic
  trilateral meeting with Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin on Thursday.
- Sources: Associated Press ("Trump arrives in Philippines to kick off
  Asia trip with economic deals and talks with allies"); Metropolitan.ph,
  "Quad Leaders Tackle Indo-Pacific Security In 1st Leader-Level Talks
  In Philippines" (published 2026-11-18T00:55:55Z); New York Post.

## 2. Ukraine cripples Crimean Bridge; Russia hits Kyiv bridges
- Ukraine struck the bridge linking Russia and Crimea for a second
  straight day; the central road arch collapsed into the Kerch Strait,
  traffic suspended, two killed (per Crimea's Russian-installed
  governor). Russia struck Kyiv's North and South bridges over the
  Dnipro and damaged the metro; largest Ukrainian drone attack of the war.
- Sources: Reuters, "Ukraine strikes Crimean bridge for second day as
  Russia pounds Kyiv bridges" (wrapup published 2026-11-18 04:51 IST);
  The Irish Times; DW ("Ukraine updates: Key bridges in Kyiv, Crimea
  hit in strikes").

## 3. Iran drones target US radar in Bahrain — intercepted
- Iran's IRGC said combat drones "precisely targeted" a US radar system
  at Sheikh Isa Air Base, Bahrain. Bahrain and US Central Command said
  the drones were intercepted with no damage to US equipment and no
  injuries — the third Iran-claimed attack on US radar in Bahrain in
  five days. US fighter jets reportedly struck an IRGC missile launch
  site on Qeshm Island in response.
- Sources: Al Jazeera, "Bahrain thwarts drone attack; Iran claims
  targeting US radar" (2026-11-17T23:52Z); particle.news, "U.S. Says
  Drones Intercepted After Iran Claims Strike on Bahrain Radar"
  (published 2026-11-18T00:01:50Z); TBS News / Reuters.

## 4. Nvidia sales double; board says Huang will not return
- Nvidia reported quarterly revenue of $115.84bn, up 103% year on year
  and above forecasts, in its first results since co-founder Jensen
  Huang resigned as CEO (12 Nov). Board chair Harvey Jones said the
  board "does not intend to reinstate Jensen as CEO", despite Huang
  saying he would be honoured to return. CFO Colette Kress is interim
  CEO; Q4 guided to ~$130bn.
- Sources: CNN, "Nvidia rules out Jensen Huang's return as CEO";
  Gizmodo (published 2026-11-17T21:46Z); USA Today.

## 5. UK sanctions eight Israeli settlement firms
- Britain imposed asset freezes and director disqualifications on eight
  Israeli construction/property firms involved in building West Bank
  settlements, and sanctioned the Tzav 9 group for blocking Gaza aid
  convoys. Foreign Secretary Ed Miliband: protecting the two-state
  solution is "the north star". Announced two days before E1 tender
  deadlines; Israel promised countermeasures.
- Sources: BBC / Reuters, "Britain sanctions Israeli settlement firms
  in push to protect two-state solution"; realnewshub (published
  2026-11-18T00:43Z); Brownstone Worldwide (published 2026-11-18T00:39Z).

## 6. Cyclone Akvan bears down on India
- Cyclonic Storm Akvan over the Bay of Bengal, ~610 km east of Chennai,
  intensifying into a Severe Cyclonic Storm on 18 Nov and a Very Severe
  Cyclonic Storm by 19 Nov; forecast landfall on the Andhra Pradesh
  coast between Nellore and Tuni around noon 20 Nov with sustained
  winds 120–130 km/h, gusts to 145 km/h; heavy to extremely heavy rain
  and 1–2 m storm surge warned.
- Sources: India Meteorological Department, National Bulletin No. 14
  (BOB/10/2026), issued 13:00 IST 17 Nov 2026; Dainik News Live;
  thelibraryestate.com.
